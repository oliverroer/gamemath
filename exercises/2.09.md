## Exercise

Evaluate the following vector expressions>

1. $\begin{bmatrix}2 \\ 6\end{bmatrix} \cdot \begin{bmatrix}-3 \\ 8\end{bmatrix}$
2. $-7\begin{bmatrix}1 & 2\end{bmatrix} \cdot \begin{bmatrix}11 & -4\end{bmatrix}$
3. $10 + \begin{bmatrix}-5 \\ 1 \\ 3\end{bmatrix} \cdot \begin{bmatrix}4 \\ -13 \\ 9\end{bmatrix}$
4. $3\begin{bmatrix}-2 \\ 0 \\ 4\end{bmatrix} \cdot \left(\begin{bmatrix}8 \\ -2 \\ 3/2\end{bmatrix} + \begin{bmatrix}0 \\ 9 \\ 7\end{bmatrix} \right)$

## Solution

```math
\begin{bmatrix}2 \\ 6\end{bmatrix} \cdot \begin{bmatrix}-3 \\ 8\end{bmatrix}
=
2 * -3 + 6 * 8
=
-6 + 48
\\=
42
```

---

```math
-7\begin{bmatrix}1 & 2\end{bmatrix} \cdot \begin{bmatrix}11 & -4\end{bmatrix}
=
\begin{bmatrix}-7 & -14\end{bmatrix} \cdot \begin{bmatrix}11 & -4\end{bmatrix}
=
-7*11 + -14*-4
=
-77 + 56
\\=
-21

```

---

```math
10 + \begin{bmatrix}-5 \\ 1 \\ 3\end{bmatrix} \cdot \begin{bmatrix}4 \\ -13 \\ 9\end{bmatrix}
=
10 + -5*4 + 1*-13 + 3*9
=
10 - 20 - 13 + 27
\\=
4
```

---

```math
3\begin{bmatrix}-2 \\ 0 \\ 4\end{bmatrix} \cdot \left(\begin{bmatrix}8 \\ -2 \\ 3/2\end{bmatrix} + \begin{bmatrix}0 \\ 9 \\ 7\end{bmatrix} \right)
=
\begin{bmatrix}3*-2 \\ 3*0 \\ 3*4\end{bmatrix}
\cdot
\begin{bmatrix}8+0 \\ -2+9 \\ 1.5+7\end{bmatrix}
=
\begin{bmatrix}-6 \\ 0 \\ 12\end{bmatrix}
\cdot
\begin{bmatrix}8 \\ 7 \\ 8.5\end{bmatrix}
=
-6*8 + 0*7 + 12*8.5
=
-48 + 0 + 102
\\=
54
```
