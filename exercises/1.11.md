## Exercise

Confirm the following:

```math
\begin{aligned}
&(a)\space
(\sin(\alpha)/\csc(\alpha)) + (\cos(\alpha) / \sec(\alpha)) = 1

\\
&(b)\space
(\sec^2(\theta) - 1) / \sec^2(\theta) = \sin^2(\theta)

\\
&(c)\space
1 + \cot^2(t) = \csc^2(t)

\\
&(d)\space
\cos(\phi)(\tan(\phi) + \cot(\phi)) = \csc(\phi)
\end{aligned}
```

## Solution

Recap:

```math
\begin{aligned}

\sec(\alpha) &= 1 / \cos(\alpha)
\\
\tan(\alpha) &= \sin(\alpha) / \cos(\alpha)
\\
\csc(\alpha) &= 1 / \sin(\alpha)
\\
\cot(\alpha) &= 1 / \tan(\alpha) = \cos(\alpha) / \sin(\alpha)
\\
\sin(\alpha)^2 + \cos(\alpha)^2 &= 1
\end{aligned}
```

---

```math
(a)\space
(\sin(\alpha)/\csc(\alpha)) + (\cos(\alpha) / \sec(\alpha)) = 1
```

```math
\begin{aligned}

(\sin(\alpha)/\csc(\alpha)) + (\cos(\alpha) / \sec(\alpha)) &= 1

\\
(\sin(\alpha)/(1 / \sin(\alpha))) + (\cos(\alpha) / (1 / \cos(\alpha))) &= 1

\\
(\sin(\alpha)*\sin(\alpha)) + (\cos(\alpha) * \cos(\alpha)) &= 1

\\
\sin(\alpha)^2 + \cos(\alpha)^2 &= 1

\end{aligned}
```

---

```math
(b)\space
(\sec^2(\theta) - 1) / \sec^2(\theta) = \sin^2(\theta)
```

```math
\begin{aligned}

(\sec^2(\theta) - 1) / \sec^2(\theta) &= \sin^2(\theta)

\\
((\sec(\theta))^2 - 1) / (\sec(\theta))^2 &= (\sin(\theta))^2

\\
((1 / \cos(\theta))^2 - 1) / (1 / \cos(\theta))^2 &= (\sin(\theta))^2

\\
((1 / \cos(\theta))^2 - 1) * (\cos(\theta))^2 &= (\sin(\theta))^2

\\
((\cos(\theta))^2 / (\cos(\theta))^2) - (\cos(\theta))^2 &= (\sin(\theta))^2

\\
1 - \cos(\theta)^2 &= \sin(\theta)^2

\\
1 &= \sin(\theta)^2 + \cos(\theta)^2
\end{aligned}
```

---

```math
(c)\space
1 + \cot^2(t) = \csc^2(t)
```

```math
\begin{aligned}

1 + \cot^2(t) &= \csc^2(t)

\\
1 + \cot(t)^2 &= \csc(t)^2

\\
1 + \frac{1}{\tan(t)^2} &= \csc(t)^2

\\
\frac{\tan(t)^2}{\tan(t)^2} + \frac{1}{\tan(t)^2} &= \csc(t)^2

\\
\frac{1 + \tan(t)^2}{\tan(t)^2} &= \csc(t)^2

\\
\frac{1 + (\sin(t) / \cos(t))^2}{(\sin(t) / \cos(t))^2} &= \csc(t)^2

\\
\frac{1 + (\sin(t) / \cos(t))^2}{(\sin(t)^2 / \cos(t)^2)} &= \csc(t)^2

\\
(1 + (\sin(t) / \cos(t))^2) * \frac{\cos(t)^2}{\sin(t)^2} &= \csc(t)^2

\\
\frac{(1 + (\sin(t) / \cos(t))^2) * \cos(t)^2}{\sin(t)^2} &= \csc(t)^2

\\
\frac{(1 + \sin(t)^2 / \cos(t)^2) * \cos(t)^2}{\sin(t)^2} &= \csc(t)^2

\\
\frac{\cos(t)^2 + \sin(t)^2}{\sin(t)^2} &= \csc(t)^2

\\
\frac{1}{\sin(t)^2} &= \csc(t)^2

\\
(\frac{1}{\sin(t)})^2 &= \csc(t)^2

\end{aligned}
```

---

```math
(d)\space
\cos(\phi)(\tan(\phi) + \cot(\phi)) = \csc(\phi)
```


```math
\begin{aligned}

\cos(\phi)(\tan(\phi) + \cot(\phi)) &= \csc(\phi)

\\
\cos(\phi)(\frac{\sin(\phi)}{\cos(\phi)} + \frac{\cos(\phi)}{\sin(\phi)}) &= \csc(\phi)

\\
\frac{\cos(\phi)\sin(\phi)}{\cos(\phi)} + \frac{\cos(\phi)\cos(\phi)}{\sin(\phi)} &= \csc(\phi)

\\
\sin(\phi) + \frac{\cos(\phi)^2}{\sin(\phi)} &= \csc(\phi)

\\
\frac {\sin(\phi)^2}{\sin(\phi)} + \frac{\cos(\phi)^2}{\sin(\phi)} &= \csc(\phi)

\\
\frac {\sin(\phi)^2 + \cos(\phi)^2}{\sin(\phi)} &= \csc(\phi)

\\
\frac {1}{\sin(\phi)} &= \csc(\phi)

\end{aligned}
```
