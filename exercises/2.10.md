## Exercise

Given the two vectors

```math
\textbf{v} = \begin{bmatrix}4 \\ 3 \\ -1\end{bmatrix}
,
\hat{\textbf{n}} = \begin{bmatrix}\sqrt2/2 \\ \sqrt2/2 \\ 0\end{bmatrix}
```

separate $\textbf{v}$ into components that are perpendicular and parallel to $\hat{\textbf{n}}$. (As the notation implies, $\hat{\textbf{n}}$ is a unit vector.)

## Solution

```math
\begin{aligned}

\textbf{v}_\parallel 

&= (\hat{\textbf{n}} \cdot \textbf{v})\hat{\textbf{n}}

\\&=
\left(
    \begin{bmatrix}\sqrt2/2 \\ \sqrt2/2 \\ 0\end{bmatrix}
    \cdot
    \begin{bmatrix}4 \\ 3 \\ -1\end{bmatrix}
\right)
\begin{bmatrix}\sqrt2/2 \\ \sqrt2/2 \\ 0\end{bmatrix}

\\&=
(
    \sqrt2/2*4 + \sqrt2/2*3 + 0*-1
)
\begin{bmatrix}\sqrt2/2 \\ \sqrt2/2 \\ 0\end{bmatrix}

\\&=
(
    2\sqrt2 + 1.5\sqrt2
)
\begin{bmatrix}\sqrt2/2 \\ \sqrt2/2 \\ 0\end{bmatrix}

\\&=
3.5\sqrt2 \begin{bmatrix}\sqrt2/2 \\ \sqrt2/2 \\ 0\end{bmatrix}

\\&=
\begin{bmatrix}
    3.5\sqrt2 * \sqrt2/2
        \\
    3.5\sqrt2 * \sqrt2/2
        \\
    3.5\sqrt2 * 0
\end{bmatrix}

\\&=
\begin{bmatrix}
    3.5 \\ 3.5 \\ 0
\end{bmatrix}

\end{aligned}
```

```math
\begin{aligned}

\textbf{v}_\perp 
&= \textbf{v} - \textbf{v}_\parallel

\\&=
\begin{bmatrix}
    4 \\ 3 \\ -1
\end{bmatrix}
-
\begin{bmatrix}
    3.5 \\ 3.5 \\ 0
\end{bmatrix}

\\&=
\begin{bmatrix}
    4-3.5 \\ 3-3.5 \\ -1-0
    
\end{bmatrix}


\\&=
\begin{bmatrix}
    0.5 \\ -0.5 \\ -1
    
\end{bmatrix}

\end{aligned}
```
