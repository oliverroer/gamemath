## Exercise

Normalize the following vectors:

1. $\begin{bmatrix}12 & 5\end{bmatrix}$
2. $\begin{bmatrix}0 & 743.632\end{bmatrix}$
3. $\begin{bmatrix}8 & -3 & 1/2\end{bmatrix}$
4. $\begin{bmatrix}-12 & 3 & -4\end{bmatrix}$
5. $\begin{bmatrix}1 & 1 & 1 & 1\end{bmatrix}$

## Solution

```math
\begin{bmatrix}
    12 & 5
\end{bmatrix}
```
magnitude:
```math
\sqrt{12^2 + 5^2}=\sqrt{144 + 25}=\sqrt{169}
\\=13
```
normalized:
```math
\begin{bmatrix}
    12 & 5
\end{bmatrix} / 13
=
\begin{bmatrix}
    12/13 & 5/13
\end{bmatrix}
\\=
\begin{bmatrix}
    0.9230769230769231 & 0.38461538461538464
\end{bmatrix}
```

---

```math
\begin{bmatrix}
    0 & 743.632
\end{bmatrix}
```

magnitude:
```math
\sqrt{0^2 + 743.632^2} = \sqrt{743.632^2}
\\= 743.632
```

normalized:
```math
\begin{bmatrix}
    0 & 743.632
\end{bmatrix} / 743.632
= 
\begin{bmatrix}
    0/743.632 & 743.632/743.632
\end{bmatrix}
\\= 
\begin{bmatrix}
    0 & 1
\end{bmatrix}
```

---

```math
\begin{bmatrix}
    8 & -3 & 1/2
\end{bmatrix}
```

magnitude:
```math
\sqrt{8^2 + -3^2 + 1/2^2} = 
\sqrt{64 + 9 + 1/4} 
\\= \sqrt{73.25}
```

normalized:
```math
\begin{bmatrix}
    8 & -3 & 1/2
\end{bmatrix} / \sqrt{73.25}
=
\begin{bmatrix}
    8 / \sqrt{73.25}
    &
    -3 / \sqrt{73.25}
    &
    0.5 / \sqrt{73.25}
\end{bmatrix}
\\=
\begin{bmatrix}
    0.9347299805391776
    &
    -0.3505237427021916
    &
    0.0292103118918493
\end{bmatrix}
```

```math
\begin{bmatrix}
    -12 & 3 & -4
\end{bmatrix}
```

magnitude:
```math
\sqrt{
    -12^2 + 3^2 + -4^2
}
=
\sqrt{
    144 + 9 + 16
}
=
\sqrt{
    169
}
\\=13
```

normalized:
```math
\begin{bmatrix}
    -12 & 3 & -4
\end{bmatrix} / 13
=
\begin{bmatrix}
    -12/13
    &
    3/13
    &
    -4/13
\end{bmatrix} 
\\=
\begin{bmatrix}
    -0.9230769230769231
    &
    0.23076923076923078
    &
    -0.3076923076923077
\end{bmatrix}
```

---

```math
\begin{bmatrix}
    1 & 1 & 1 & 1
\end{bmatrix}
```

magnitude:
```math
\sqrt{
    1^2 + 1^2 + 1^2 + 1^2
}
=
\sqrt{
    1 + 1 + 1 + 1
}
=
\sqrt{
    4
}
\\= 2
```

normalized:
```math
\begin{bmatrix}
    1 & 1 & 1 & 1
\end{bmatrix} /2
=
\begin{bmatrix}
    1/2 & 1/2 & 1/2 & 1/2
\end{bmatrix} 
\\=
\begin{bmatrix}
    0.5 & 0.5 & 0.5 & 0.5
\end{bmatrix} 
```
