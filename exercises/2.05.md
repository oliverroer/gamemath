## Exercise

Evaluate the following vector expressions:

1. $-\begin{bmatrix}3 & 7\end{bmatrix}$
2. $\|\begin{bmatrix}-12 & 5\end{bmatrix}\|$
3. $\|\begin{bmatrix}8 & -3 & 1/2\end{bmatrix}\|$
4. $3\begin{bmatrix}4 & -7 & 0\end{bmatrix}$
5. $\begin{bmatrix}4 & 5\end{bmatrix}/2$

## Solution

1. $-\begin{bmatrix}3 & 7\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}-3 & -7\end{bmatrix}$
2. $\|\begin{bmatrix}-12 & 5\end{bmatrix}\| = \sqrt{-12^2 + 5^2} = \sqrt{144 + 25} = \sqrt{169} = 13$
3. $\|\begin{bmatrix}8 & -3 & 1/2\end{bmatrix}\| = \sqrt{8^2 + {-3}^2 + {\frac 1 2}^2} = \sqrt{64 + 9 + \frac 1 4} = \sqrt{73.25} = 8.558621384311845$
4. $3\begin{bmatrix}4 & -7 & 0\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}3*4 & 3*-7 & 3*0\end{bmatrix}  = \begin{bmatrix}12 & -21 & 0\end{bmatrix}$
5. $\begin{bmatrix}4 & 5\end{bmatrix}/2 = \begin{bmatrix}4/2 & 5/2\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}2 & 2.5\end{bmatrix}$
