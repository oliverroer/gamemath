## Exercise

Use trigonometric identities and the algebraic definition of the dot product in 2D

```math
\textbf{a} \cdot \textbf{b} = a_xb_x + a_yb_y
```

to prove the geometric interpretation of the dot product in 2D.
> Hint: draw a diagram of the vectors and all angles involved.

## Solution

**given** $\hat{\textbf{v}} = \textbf{v} / \| \textbf{v}\|$ **then**
```math
\begin{aligned}

a \cdot b &= \|a\| \|b\| \cos \theta
\\
\frac{a}{\|a\|} \cdot \frac{b}{\|b\|} &= \cos \theta
\\
\hat{a} \cdot \hat{b} &= \cos \theta

\end{aligned}
```

---

**given** 
```math
\hat{v}
=
\begin{bmatrix}
    \cos V \\ \sin V
\end{bmatrix}
```
**where** $V$ is the angle between $\hat{v}$ and the $x$-axis

**then**
```math
\begin{aligned}

\hat{a} \cdot \hat{b} &= \cos \theta
\\
\begin{bmatrix}
    \cos A \\ \sin A
\end{bmatrix}
\cdot
\begin{bmatrix}
    \cos B \\ \sin B
\end{bmatrix}
&= \cos \theta

\end{aligned}
```
**where** $A$ is the angle beween $a$ and the $x$-axis

**and** $B$ is the angle beween $b$ and the $x$-axis

---

**given**

```math
\textbf{a} \cdot \textbf{b} = a_xb_x + a_yb_y
```

**then**

```math
\begin{aligned}

\begin{bmatrix}
    \cos A \\ \sin A
\end{bmatrix}
\cdot
\begin{bmatrix}
    \cos B \\ \sin B
\end{bmatrix} &= \cos \theta

\\
\cos A \cos B +
\sin A \sin B
&= \cos \theta

\end{aligned}
```

---

**given**
```math
\cos(a - b) = \cos a \cos b + \sin a \sin b
```
**then**
```math
\begin{aligned}


\cos A \cos B +
\sin A \sin B
&= \cos \theta

\\

\cos(A - B)
&= \cos \theta

\\

A - B
&= \theta

\end{aligned}
```

---

**given** $\theta$ is defined as the angle between vectors $a$ and $b$
**then** it seems to match up with the difference between angles $A$ and $B$.
