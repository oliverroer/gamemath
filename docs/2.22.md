## Exercise

Consider three points labeled $\textbf{a}$, $\textbf{b}$, and $\textbf{c}$ in the plane of our left-handed coordinate system, which represent waypoints on an NPC's path.

1. How can the cross product be used to determine whether, when moving from $\textbf{a}$ to $\textbf{b}$ to $\textbf{c}$, the NPC makes a clockwise or counterclockwise turn at $\textbf{b}$, when viewing the path from above? 

2. For each of the following sets of three points, determine whether the NPC is turning clockwise or counterclockwise when moving from $\textbf{a}$ to $\textbf{b}$ to $\textbf{c}$.

```math
\begin{aligned}

(1)\space
\textbf{a} &= \begin{bmatrix}
```

## Solution

We can make a vector from $\textbf{a}$ to $\textbf{b}$, and another from
$\textbf{b}$ to $\textbf{c}$, and takes the cross
product of those two.

```math
(\textbf{b} - \textbf{a}) \times (\textbf{c} - \textbf{b})
```

In a left-handed coordinate system, this cross product will point towards us
(the $y$-component is positive) when doing a clockwise turn, and away from us 
(the $y$-component is negative) when making a counterclockwise turn.

The cross product is defined as such:
```math
\begin{bmatrix}
x_1 \\
y_1 \\
z_1
\end{bmatrix}

\times

\begin{bmatrix}
x_2 \\
y_2 \\
z_2
\end{bmatrix}

=
\begin{bmatrix}
y_1 z_2 - z_1 y_2 \\
z_1 x_2 - x_1 z_2 \\
x_1 y_2 - y_1 x_2
\end{bmatrix}
```

Specifically, the $y$-component of the cross product is $z_1 x_2 - x_1 z_2$.

So, instead of doing the full cross product, we can just do the part of the $y$-
component like so

```math
(b_z - a_z) * (c_x - b_x) - (b_x - a_x) * (c_z - b_z)
```

and check the sign of the result.

---

```math
\begin{aligned}

(1)\space
\textbf{a} &=
\begin{bmatrix}
    2 &
    0 &
    3
\end{bmatrix}
,
\\
\textbf{b} &=
\begin{bmatrix}
    -1 &
    0 &
    5
\end{bmatrix}
,
\\
\textbf{c} &=
\begin{bmatrix}
    -4 &
    0 &
    1
\end{bmatrix}
,

\end{aligned}
```

```math
\begin{aligned}

(b_z - a_z) * (c_x - b_x) - (b_x - a_x) * (c_z - b_z)
&=
(5 - 3) * (-4 - (-1)) - (-1 - 2) * (1 - 5)
\\&=
2 * (-3) - (-3) * (-4)
\\&=
-6 - 12
\\&=
-18 
\\&
\implies \text{counter-clockwise turn}

\end{aligned}
```

---

```math
\begin{aligned}

(2)\space
\textbf{a} &=
\begin{bmatrix}
    -3 &
    0 &
    -5
\end{bmatrix}
,
\\
\textbf{b} &=
\begin{bmatrix}
    4 &
    0 &
    0
\end{bmatrix}
,
\\
\textbf{c} &=
\begin{bmatrix}
    3 &
    0 &
    3
\end{bmatrix}
,

\end{aligned}
```

```math
\begin{aligned}

(b_z - a_z) * (c_x - b_x) - (b_x - a_x) * (c_z - b_z)
&=
(0 - -5) * (3 - 4) - (4 - -3) * (3 - 0)
\\&=
5 * (-1) - 7 * 3
\\&=
-5 - 21
\\&=
-26
\\&
\implies \text{counter-clockwise turn}

\end{aligned}
```
---

```math
\begin{aligned}

(3)\space
\textbf{a} &= \begin{bmatrix}
    1 &
    0 &
    4
\end{bmatrix},
\\
\textbf{b} &= \begin{bmatrix}
    7 &
    0 &
    -1
\end{bmatrix},
\\
\textbf{c} &= \begin{bmatrix}
    -5 &
    0 &
    -6
\end{bmatrix},

\end{aligned}
```

```math
\begin{aligned}

(b_z - a_z) * (c_x - b_x) - (b_x - a_x) * (c_z - b_z)
&=
(-1 - 4) * (-5 - 7) - (7 - 1) * (-6 - (-1))
\\&=
-5 * (-12) - 6 * (-5)
\\&=
60 + 30
\\&=
90
\\&
\implies \text{clockwise turn}

\end{aligned}
```

---

```math
\begin{aligned}

(4)\space
\textbf{a} &= \begin{bmatrix}
    -2 &
    0 &
    1
\end{bmatrix},
\\
\textbf{b} &= \begin{bmatrix}
    1 &
    0 &
    2
\end{bmatrix},
\\
\textbf{c} &= \begin{bmatrix}
    4 &
    0 &
    4
\end{bmatrix},

\end{aligned}
```


```math
\begin{aligned}

(b_z - a_z) * (c_x - b_x) - (b_x - a_x) * (c_z - b_z)
&=
(2 - 1) * (4 - 1) - (1 - (-2)) * (4 - 2)
\\&=
1 * 3 - 3 * 2
\\&=
3 - 6
\\&=
-3
\\&
\implies \text{counter-clockwise turn}

\end{aligned}
```
