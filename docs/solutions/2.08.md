## Exercise

Compute the distance between the following pairs of points: 

1. $\begin{bmatrix}10 \\ 6\end{bmatrix}, \begin{bmatrix}-14 \\ 30\end{bmatrix}$
2. $\begin{bmatrix}0 \\ 0\end{bmatrix}, \begin{bmatrix}-12 \\ 5\end{bmatrix}$
3. $\begin{bmatrix}3 \\ 10 \\ 7\end{bmatrix}, \begin{bmatrix}8 \\ -7 \\ 4\end{bmatrix}$
4. $\begin{bmatrix}-2 \\ -4 \\ 9\end{bmatrix}, \begin{bmatrix}6 \\ -7 \\ -9.5\end{bmatrix}$
5. $\begin{bmatrix}4 \\ -4 \\ -4 \\ 4\end{bmatrix}, \begin{bmatrix}-6 \\ 6 \\ 6 \\ -6\end{bmatrix}$

## Solution

```math
\|
\begin{bmatrix}10 \\ 6\end{bmatrix} - \begin{bmatrix}-14 \\ 30\end{bmatrix}
\|
\\=
\|
\begin{bmatrix}10+14 \\ 6-30\end{bmatrix}
\|
\\=
\|
\begin{bmatrix}24 \\ -24\end{bmatrix}
\|
\\=
\sqrt{24^2 + (-24)^2}
\\=
\sqrt{576 + 576}
\\=
\sqrt{1152}
\\=
33.941125497
```

```math
\|
\begin{bmatrix}0 \\ 0\end{bmatrix} - \begin{bmatrix}-12 \\ 5\end{bmatrix}
\|
\\=
\|
\begin{bmatrix}12 \\ -5\end{bmatrix}
\|
\\=
\sqrt{12^2 + (-5)^2}
\\=
\sqrt{144 + 25}
\\=
\sqrt{169}
\\=
13
```

---

```math
\|
\begin{bmatrix}3 \\ 10 \\ 7\end{bmatrix} - \begin{bmatrix}8 \\ -7 \\ 4\end{bmatrix}
\|
\\=
\|
\begin{bmatrix}3-8 \\ 10+7 \\ 7-4\end{bmatrix}
\|
\\=
\|
\begin{bmatrix}-5 \\ 17 \\ 3\end{bmatrix}
\|
\\=
\sqrt{
    (-5)^2 + 17^2 + 3^2
}
\\=
\sqrt{
    25 + 289 + 9
}
\\=
\sqrt{323}
\\=
\sqrt{323}
\\=
17.972200756
```

```math
\|
\begin{bmatrix}-2 \\ -4 \\ 9\end{bmatrix} - \begin{bmatrix}6 \\ -7 \\ 9.5\end{bmatrix}
\|
\\=
\|
\begin{bmatrix}-2-6 \\ -4+7 \\ 9-9.5\end{bmatrix}
\|
\\=
\|
\begin{bmatrix}-8 \\ 3 \\ -0.5\end{bmatrix}
\|
\\=
\sqrt{
    (-8)^2 + 3^2 + (-0.5)^2
}
\\=
\sqrt{
    64 + 9 + 0.25
}
\\=
\sqrt{
    73.25
}
\\=
8.558621384
```

```math
\|
\begin{bmatrix}4 \\ -4 \\ -4 \\ 4\end{bmatrix} - \begin{bmatrix}-6 \\ 6 \\ 6 \\ -6\end{bmatrix}
\|
\\=
\|
\begin{bmatrix}4+6 \\ -4-6 \\ -4-6 \\ 4+6\end{bmatrix}
\|
\\=
\|
\begin{bmatrix}10 \\ -10 \\ -10 \\ 10\end{bmatrix}
\|
\\=
\sqrt{
    10^2 + (-10)^2 + (-10)^2 + 10^2
}
\\=
\sqrt{
    100 + 100 + 100 + 100
}
\\=
\sqrt{
    400
}
\\=
20
```
