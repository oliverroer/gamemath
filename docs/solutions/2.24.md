## Exercise

A similar problem arises with the derivation of a matrix to rotate about an
arbitrary axis.

Given an arbitrary scalar $\theta$ and a vector $\textbf{n}$, substitute 
$\textbf{p} = \begin{bmatrix}1 \\ 0 \\ 0\end{bmatrix}$
and simplify the value of $\textbf{p}'$ in the expression.

```math
\textbf{p}' =
\cos \theta 
(\textbf{p} - (\textbf{p} \cdot \textbf{n})\textbf{n})
+ \sin \theta (\textbf{n} \times \textbf{p})
+ (\textbf{p} \cdot \textbf{n})\textbf{n}
```

## Solution

```math
\textbf{p} = \begin{bmatrix}1 \\ 0 \\ 0\end{bmatrix},
```

```math
\begin{aligned}

\textbf{p}' 
\\=
\cos \theta 
(
    \textbf{p}
    -
    (
        \textbf{p} \cdot \textbf{n}
    )
    \textbf{n}
)
+
\sin \theta (
    \textbf{n} \times \textbf{p}
)
+
(
    \textbf{p} \cdot \textbf{n}
)
\textbf{n}

\\\\=
\cos \theta 
(
    \textbf{p}
    -
    n_x\textbf{n}
)
+
\sin \theta (
    \textbf{n} \times \textbf{p}
)
+
n_x\textbf{n}

\\\\=
\cos \theta 
\left(
    \textbf{p}
    -
    \begin{bmatrix}
        n_x^2 \\
        n_x n_y \\
        n_x n_z
    \end{bmatrix}
\right)
+
\sin \theta (
    \textbf{n} \times \textbf{p}
)
+
\begin{bmatrix}
    n_x^2 \\
    n_x n_y \\
    n_x n_z
\end{bmatrix}

\\\\=
\cos \theta 
\left(
    \begin{bmatrix}1 \\ 0 \\ 0\end{bmatrix}
    -
    \begin{bmatrix}
        n_x^2 \\
        n_x n_y \\
        n_x n_z
    \end{bmatrix}
\right)
+
\sin \theta (
    \textbf{n} \times \textbf{p}
)
+
\begin{bmatrix}
    n_x^2 \\
    n_x n_y \\
    n_x n_z
\end{bmatrix}

\\\\=
\cos \theta 
\begin{bmatrix}
    1 - n_x^2 \\
    0 - n_x n_y \\
    0 - n_x n_z
\end{bmatrix}
+
\sin \theta (
    \textbf{n} \times \textbf{p}
)
+
\begin{bmatrix}
    n_x^2 \\
    n_x n_y \\
    n_x n_z
\end{bmatrix}

\\\\=
\cos \theta 
\begin{bmatrix}
    1 - n_x^2 \\
    -n_x n_y \\
    -n_x n_z
\end{bmatrix}
+
\sin \theta (
    \textbf{n} \times \textbf{p}
)
+
\begin{bmatrix}
    n_x^2 \\
    n_x n_y \\
    n_x n_z
\end{bmatrix}

\\\\=
\begin{bmatrix}
    \cos \theta (1 - n_x^2) \\
    - \cos \theta n_x n_y \\
    - \cos \theta n_x n_z
\end{bmatrix}
+
\sin \theta (
    \textbf{n} \times \textbf{p}
)
+
\begin{bmatrix}
    n_x^2 \\
    n_x n_y \\
    n_x n_z
\end{bmatrix}

\\\\=
\begin{bmatrix}
    \cos \theta (1 - n_x^2) \\
    - \cos \theta n_x n_y \\
    - \cos \theta n_x n_z
\end{bmatrix}
+
\sin \theta
\left(
    \begin{bmatrix}
        n_x \\
        n_y \\
        n_z
    \end{bmatrix}
    \times
    \begin{bmatrix}
        1 \\
        0 \\
        0
    \end{bmatrix}
\right)
+
\begin{bmatrix}
    n_x^2 \\
    n_x n_y \\
    n_x n_z
\end{bmatrix}

\\\\=
\begin{bmatrix}
    \cos \theta (1 - n_x^2) \\
    - \cos \theta n_x n_y \\
    - \cos \theta n_x n_z
\end{bmatrix}
+
\sin \theta
\begin{bmatrix}
    n_y * 0 - n_z * 0 \\
    n_z * 1 - n_x * 0 \\
    n_x * 0 - n_y * 1
\end{bmatrix}
+
\begin{bmatrix}
    n_x^2 \\
    n_x n_y \\
    n_x n_z
\end{bmatrix}

\\\\=
\begin{bmatrix}
    \cos \theta (1 - n_x^2) \\
    - \cos \theta n_x n_y \\
    - \cos \theta n_x n_z
\end{bmatrix}
+
\sin \theta
\begin{bmatrix}
    0 \\
    n_z \\
    -n_y
\end{bmatrix}
+
\begin{bmatrix}
    n_x^2 \\
    n_x n_y \\
    n_x n_z
\end{bmatrix}

\\\\=
\begin{bmatrix}
    \cos \theta (1 - n_x^2) \\
    - \cos \theta n_x n_y \\
    - \cos \theta n_x n_z
\end{bmatrix}
+
\begin{bmatrix}
    \sin \theta * 0 \\
    \sin \theta * n_z \\
    \sin \theta * -n_y
\end{bmatrix}
+
\begin{bmatrix}
    n_x^2 \\
    n_x n_y \\
    n_x n_z
\end{bmatrix}

\\\\=
\begin{bmatrix}
    \cos \theta (1 - n_x^2) \\
    - \cos \theta n_x n_y \\
    - \cos \theta n_x n_z
\end{bmatrix}
+
\begin{bmatrix}
    0 \\
    \sin \theta n_z \\
    - \sin \theta n_y
\end{bmatrix}
+
\begin{bmatrix}
    n_x^2 \\
    n_x n_y \\
    n_x n_z
\end{bmatrix}

\\\\=
\begin{bmatrix}
    \cos \theta (1 - n_x^2) + 0 + n_x^2
        \\
    - \cos \theta n_x n_y + \sin \theta n_z + n_x n_y
        \\
    - \cos \theta n_x n_z + - \sin \theta n_y + n_x n_z
\end{bmatrix}

\\\\=
\begin{bmatrix}
    \cos \theta (1 - n_x^2) + n_x^2
        \\
    n_x n_y - \cos \theta n_x n_y + \sin \theta n_z
        \\
    n_x n_z - \cos \theta n_x n_z - \sin \theta n_y
\end{bmatrix}

\\\\=
\begin{bmatrix}
    \cos \theta (1 - n_x^2) + n_x^2
        \\
    (1 - \cos \theta) n_x n_y + \sin \theta n_z
        \\
    (1 - \cos \theta) n_x n_z - \sin \theta n_y
\end{bmatrix}

\\\\=
\begin{bmatrix}
     \cos \theta - \cos \theta n_x^2 + n_x^2
        \\
    (1 - \cos \theta) n_x n_y + \sin \theta n_z
        \\
    (1 - \cos \theta) n_x n_z - \sin \theta n_y
\end{bmatrix}

\\\\=
\begin{bmatrix}
     \cos \theta + n_x^2 - \cos \theta n_x^2
        \\
    (1 - \cos \theta) n_x n_y + \sin \theta n_z
        \\
    (1 - \cos \theta) n_x n_z - \sin \theta n_y
\end{bmatrix}

\\\\=
\begin{bmatrix}
     \cos \theta + n_x^2 (1 - \cos \theta)
        \\
    (1 - \cos \theta) n_x n_y + \sin \theta n_z
        \\
    (1 - \cos \theta) n_x n_z - \sin \theta n_y
\end{bmatrix}

\\\\=
\begin{bmatrix}
    (1 - \cos \theta) n_x^2 + \cos \theta
        \\
    (1 - \cos \theta) n_x n_y + \sin \theta n_z
        \\
    (1 - \cos \theta) n_x n_z - \sin \theta n_y
\end{bmatrix}

\\\\=
\begin{bmatrix}
    n_x^2 (1 - \cos \theta) + \cos \theta
        \\
    n_x n_y (1 - \cos \theta) + n_z \sin \theta
        \\
    n_x n_z (1 - \cos \theta) - n_y \sin \theta
\end{bmatrix}


\end{aligned}
```
