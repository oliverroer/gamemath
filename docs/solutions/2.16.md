## Exercise

A man is boarding a plane. The airline has a rule that no carry-on item may be more than two feet long, two feet wide, or two feet tall. He has a very valuable sword that is three feet long, yet he is able to carry the sword on board with him.How is he able to do this? What is the longest possible item that he could carry on?

## Solution

None of the 3 dimensions must exceed 2 feet.

This means all three can be up to 2 feet each, and still comply.

If his item can fit in a 2x2x2 feet box, he should be fine.

The longest item that can fit in such a box would have to be less or equal to

```math
\sqrt{2^2 + 2^2 + 2^2} = 
\sqrt{4 + 4 + 4} = 
\sqrt{12} = 3.464101615
```

His sword is 3 feet, so it's all good.
