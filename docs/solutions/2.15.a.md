## Exercise

For each of the following find $\|\textbf{x}\|_1$, $\|\textbf{x}\|_2$, $\|\textbf{x}\|_3$, and $\|\textbf{x}\|_\infty$:

1. $\begin{bmatrix}3 & 4\end{bmatrix}$
2. $\begin{bmatrix}5 & -12\end{bmatrix}$
3. $\begin{bmatrix}-2 & 10 & -7\end{bmatrix}$
4. $\begin{bmatrix}6 & 1 & -9\end{bmatrix}$
5. $\begin{bmatrix}-2 & -2 & -2 & -2\end{bmatrix}$

## Solution

```math
\|\textbf{x}\|_p
\equiv
\left(
\sum_{i=1}^{n} |x_i|^p
\right)
^{1/p}
```

```math
\begin{aligned}

\|\textbf{x}\|_1
&\equiv
\left(
\sum_{i=1}^{n} |x_i|^1
\right)
^{1/1}
\\&=
\sum_{i=1}^{n} |x_i|

\end{aligned}
```

```math
\begin{aligned}

\|\textbf{x}\|_2
&\equiv
\left(
\sum_{i=1}^{n} |x_i|^2
\right)
^{1/2}
\\&=
\sqrt{
    \sum_{i=1}^{n} |x_i|^2
}

\end{aligned}
```

```math
\begin{aligned}

\|\textbf{x}\|_3
&\equiv
\left(
\sum_{i=1}^{n} |x_i|^3
\right)
^{1/3}
\\&=
\sqrt[3]{
    \sum_{i=1}^{n} |x_i|^3
}

\end{aligned}
```

```math
\begin{aligned}

\|\textbf{x}\|_\infty
&\equiv
\max(|x_1|,\dots,|x_n|)

\end{aligned}
```


---

```math
\|
\begin{bmatrix}3 & 4\end{bmatrix}
\|_1
= 3 + 4 = 7
```

```math
\|
\begin{bmatrix}3 & 4\end{bmatrix}
\|_2
= \sqrt{3^2 + 4^2} = \sqrt{9 + 16}
= \sqrt{25} = 5
```

```math
\|
\begin{bmatrix}3 & 4\end{bmatrix}
\|_3
=\sqrt[3]{3^3 + 4^3} 
=\sqrt[3]{27 + 64}
=\sqrt[3]{91}
=4.497941445
```

```math
\|
\begin{bmatrix}3 & 4\end{bmatrix}
\|_\infty
=max(3,4)=4
```
