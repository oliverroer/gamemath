## Exercise

In the derivation of a matrix to scale along an arbitrary axis, we reach a step where we have the vector expression 

```math
\textbf{p}' = \textbf{p} + (k - 1)(\textbf{p} \cdot \textbf{n})\textbf{n}
```

where $\textbf{n}$ is an arbitrary vector
$\begin{bmatrix}n_x & n_y & n_z\end{bmatrix}$ and $k$ is an arbitrary scalar,
but $\textbf{p}$ is one of the cardinal axes.

Plug in the value $\textbf{p} = \begin{bmatrix}1 & 0 & 0\end{bmatrix}$ and
simplify the resulting expression for $\textbf{p}'$.

The answer is not a vector expression, but a single vector, where the scalar
expressions for each coordinate have been simplified.

## Solution

```math
\textbf{p} = \begin{bmatrix}1 & 0 & 0\end{bmatrix},
```

```math
\begin{aligned}

\textbf{p}'
&=
\textbf{p} + (k - 1)(\textbf{p} \cdot \textbf{n})\textbf{n}

\\&=
\begin{bmatrix}1 & 0 & 0\end{bmatrix}
+
(k - 1)
\left(
    \begin{bmatrix}1 & 0 & 0\end{bmatrix}
    \cdot
    \begin{bmatrix}n_x & n_y & n_z\end{bmatrix}
    
\right)
\begin{bmatrix}n_x & n_y & n_z\end{bmatrix}

\\&=
\begin{bmatrix}1 & 0 & 0\end{bmatrix}
+
(k - 1)
\left(
    1 * n_x + 0 * n_y + 0 * n_z
\right)
\begin{bmatrix}n_x & n_y & n_z\end{bmatrix}

\\&=
\begin{bmatrix}1 & 0 & 0\end{bmatrix}
+
(k - 1) * n_x *
\begin{bmatrix}n_x & n_y & n_z\end{bmatrix}

\\&=
\begin{bmatrix}1 & 0 & 0\end{bmatrix}
+
\begin{bmatrix}
    (k - 1) n_x^2 &
    (k - 1) n_x n_y &
    (k - 1) n_x n_z
\end{bmatrix}

\\&=
\begin{bmatrix}
    1 + (k - 1) n_x^2 &
    0 + (k - 1) n_x n_y &
    0 + (k - 1) n_x n_z
\end{bmatrix}

\\&=
\begin{bmatrix}
    1 + (k - 1) n_x^2 &
    (k - 1) n_x n_y &
    (k - 1) n_x n_z
\end{bmatrix}


\end{aligned}
```
