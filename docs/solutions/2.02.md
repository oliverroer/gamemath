## Exercise

Identify the quantities in each of the following sentences as scalar or vector. For vector quantities, give the magnitude and direction. (Note: some directions may be implicit.)

1. How much do you weigh?
2. Do you have any idea how fast you were going?
3. It's two blocks north of here.
4. We're cruising from Los Angeles to New York at 600 mph, at an altitude of 33,000 ft.


## Solution

1. scalar
2. scalar
3. vector, magnitude=2 blocks, direction=north
4. vector, magnitude=600 mph, direction=NY-LA
