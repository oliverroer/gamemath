## Exercise

One common way of defining a bounding box for a 2D object is to specify a center point and a radius vector , where each component of is half the length of the side of the bounding box along the corresponding axis.

1. Describe the four corners $\textbf{P}_\text{UpperLeft}$, $\textbf{P}_\text{UpperRight}$, $\textbf{P}_\text{LowerLeft}$ and $\textbf{P}_\text{LowerRight}$
2. Describe the eight corners of a bounding cube, extending this idea into 3D.

## Solution

```math
\begin{aligned}

\textbf{P}_\text{UpperLeft} &= \begin{bmatrix}c_x-r_x \\ c_y + r_y\end{bmatrix}
,
\textbf{P}_\text{UpperRight} &= \begin{bmatrix}c_x+r_x \\ c_y + r_y\end{bmatrix}
\\
\textbf{P}_\text{LowerLeft} &= \begin{bmatrix}c_x-r_x \\ c_y - r_y\end{bmatrix}
,
\textbf{P}_\text{LowerRight} &= \begin{bmatrix}c_x+r_x \\ c_y - r_y\end{bmatrix}

\end{aligned}
```
