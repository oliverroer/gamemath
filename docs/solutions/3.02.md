## Exercise

Suppose the world axes are transformed to our object axes by rotating them counterclockwise around the $y$-axis by 42$\degree$, then translating 6 units along the $z$-axis and 12 units along the $x$-axis. Describe this transformation from the perspective of a point on the object. 

## Solution

From the point of view of the object, it has been translated -6 units
along the $z$-xis and -12 units along the $x$-axis, then rotated clockwise around the $y$-axis by $42\degree$.

Note that assumes the transformation is atomic.

If were to describe each step of the transformation separately, then
the answer would be more complicated.
