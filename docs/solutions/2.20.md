## Exercise

A nonplayer character (NPC) is standing at location $\textbf{p}$ with a forward direction of $\textbf{v}$.

1. How can the dot product be used to determine whether the point  $\textbf{x}$ is in front of or behind the NPC?

2. Let $\textbf{p} = \begin{bmatrix}-3 & 4\end{bmatrix}$
and $\textbf{v} = \begin{bmatrix}5 & -2\end{bmatrix}$. For each of the following points $\textbf{x}$ determine whether $\textbf{x}$ is in front of or behind the NPC:

    1. $\textbf{x} = \begin{bmatrix} 0 &  0  \end{bmatrix}$
    2. $\textbf{x} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 &  6  \end{bmatrix}$
    3. $\textbf{x} = \begin{bmatrix}-6 &  0  \end{bmatrix}$
    4. $\textbf{x} = \begin{bmatrix}-4 &  7  \end{bmatrix}$
    5. $\textbf{x} = \begin{bmatrix} 5 &  5  \end{bmatrix}$
    6. $\textbf{x} = \begin{bmatrix}-3 &  0  \end{bmatrix}$
    7. $\textbf{x} = \begin{bmatrix}-6 & -3.5\end{bmatrix}$

## Solution

Calculate a vector $\textbf{px}$ from $\textbf{p}$ to $\textbf{x}$, by subtracting $\textbf{p}$ from $\textbf{x}$, then calculate $\textbf{px} \cdot \textbf{v}$. The player is facing $\textbf{x}$ if the result is greater than $0$.

---

1. $\left(\begin{bmatrix} 0 &  0  \end{bmatrix} - \begin{bmatrix}-3 & 4\end{bmatrix}\right) \cdot \begin{bmatrix}5 & -2\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 0 + 3 &  0   - 4\end{bmatrix} \cdot \begin{bmatrix}5 & -2\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 3 & -4  \end{bmatrix} \cdot \begin{bmatrix}5 & -2\end{bmatrix} =  3 * 5 + (-4  ) * (-2) =  23 \implies front$
2. $\left(\begin{bmatrix} 1 &  6  \end{bmatrix} - \begin{bmatrix}-3 & 4\end{bmatrix}\right) \cdot \begin{bmatrix}5 & -2\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 + 3 &  6   - 4\end{bmatrix} \cdot \begin{bmatrix}5 & -2\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 4 &  2  \end{bmatrix} \cdot \begin{bmatrix}5 & -2\end{bmatrix} =  4 * 5 +   2    * (-2) =  16 \implies front$
3. $\left(\begin{bmatrix}-6 &  0  \end{bmatrix} - \begin{bmatrix}-3 & 4\end{bmatrix}\right) \cdot \begin{bmatrix}5 & -2\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}-6 + 3 &  0   - 4\end{bmatrix} \cdot \begin{bmatrix}5 & -2\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}-3 & -4  \end{bmatrix} \cdot \begin{bmatrix}5 & -2\end{bmatrix} = -3 * 5 + (-4  ) * (-2) =  -7 \implies behind$
4. $\left(\begin{bmatrix}-4 &  7  \end{bmatrix} - \begin{bmatrix}-3 & 4\end{bmatrix}\right) \cdot \begin{bmatrix}5 & -2\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}-4 + 3 &  7   - 4\end{bmatrix} \cdot \begin{bmatrix}5 & -2\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}-1 &  3  \end{bmatrix} \cdot \begin{bmatrix}5 & -2\end{bmatrix} = -1 * 5 +   3    * (-2) = -11 \implies behind$
5. $\left(\begin{bmatrix} 5 &  5  \end{bmatrix} - \begin{bmatrix}-3 & 4\end{bmatrix}\right) \cdot \begin{bmatrix}5 & -2\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 5 + 3 &  5   - 4\end{bmatrix} \cdot \begin{bmatrix}5 & -2\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 8 &  1  \end{bmatrix} \cdot \begin{bmatrix}5 & -2\end{bmatrix} =  8 * 5 +   1    * (-2) =  38 \implies front$
6. $\left(\begin{bmatrix}-3 &  0  \end{bmatrix} - \begin{bmatrix}-3 & 4\end{bmatrix}\right) \cdot \begin{bmatrix}5 & -2\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}-3 + 3 &  0   - 4\end{bmatrix} \cdot \begin{bmatrix}5 & -2\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 0 & -4  \end{bmatrix} \cdot \begin{bmatrix}5 & -2\end{bmatrix} =  0 * 5 + (-4  ) * (-2) =   8 \implies front$
7. $\left(\begin{bmatrix}-6 & -3.5\end{bmatrix} - \begin{bmatrix}-3 & 4\end{bmatrix}\right) \cdot \begin{bmatrix}5 & -2\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}-6 + 3 & -3.5 - 4\end{bmatrix} \cdot \begin{bmatrix}5 & -2\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}-3 & -7.5\end{bmatrix} \cdot \begin{bmatrix}5 & -2\end{bmatrix} = -3 * 5 + (-7.5) * (-2) =   0 \implies side$
