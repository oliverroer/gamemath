## Exercise

Compute the following:

```math
\begin{aligned}

&(a)\space \sum_{i=1}^{5} i
\\
&(b)\space \sum_{i=1}^{5} 2i
\\
&(c)\space \prod_{i=1}^{5} 2i
\\
&(d)\space \prod_{i=0}^{4} 7(i+1)
\\
&(e)\space \sum_{i=1}^{100} i

\end{aligned}
```

## Solution

```math
\begin{aligned}

&(a)\space 1+2+3+4+5 = 15
\\
&(b)\space 2 * 15 = 30
\\
&(c)\space 2^5 * (1*2*3*4*5) = 32 * 120 = 3850
\\
&(d)\space 7^5 * (1*2*3*4*5) = 16807 * 120 = 2016840 
\\
&(e)\space (1+100) + (2 + 99) + (3 + 98) + \ldots + (49 + 52) + (50 + 51) = 50 * 101 = 5050

\end{aligned}
```
