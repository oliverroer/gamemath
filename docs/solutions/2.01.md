## Exercise

Let

```math
a = \begin{bmatrix}
    -3 &
    8
\end{bmatrix},

b = \begin{bmatrix}
    4 \\
    0 \\
    5
\end{bmatrix},

c = \begin{bmatrix}
    16 \\
    -1 \\
    4 \\
    6
\end{bmatrix}.
```

1. Identify $a$, $b$, and $c$ as row or column vectors, and give the dimension of each vector.

2. Compute $b_y + c_w + a_x = b_z$

## Solution

1. $a$: row 2D, $b$: column 3D, $c$: column 4D
2. $0 + 6 + -3 + 5 = 8$
